# FoundersLens

FoundersLens is an AI-powered idea evaluation and research platform designed for founders, entrepreneurs, and innovators who want to assess whether their startup idea has real market potential in Pakistan and globally.

Users submit an idea through an intake form, and the system uses intelligent agents to evaluate the concept, identify weaknesses, and generate a complete market and feasibility report.

## Why FoundersLens?

Most founders struggle with two major problems:

- They have an idea but do not know whether it is viable or scalable.
- They need market research, competitor analysis, and opportunity validation before investing time and money.

FoundersLens solves this by combining:

- idea screening and validation,
- AI-assisted market research,
- feasibility and scalability analysis,
- final investor-ready insights.

## Core Idea

The system is structured in two stages:

1. Idea Evaluation Agent
   - Assesses the main concept of the idea.
   - Evaluates whether it is practical, scalable, and feasible.
   - Checks relevance in the Pakistani and global market.

2. Research and Report Agent
   - Performs deeper analysis.
   - Investigates competitors, market demand, user pain points, pricing, and positioning.
   - Produces a complete final report for the founder.

## Key Features

- Intake form for startup idea submission
- AI-based idea validation
- Market potential assessment for local and international markets
- Scalability and feasibility scoring
- Research-driven final report generation
- Entrepreneur-friendly output and recommendations
- Suitable for startup ideation and early-stage validation

## How It Works

1. User submits an idea through the intake form.
2. The system evaluates the idea concept and market relevance.
3. The first agent checks product viability and market opportunity.
4. The second agent performs research and builds a structured report.
5. The final output is delivered as a clear, actionable startup assessment.

## Project Goal

FoundersLens aims to help founders move from raw ideas to validated opportunities by providing fast, intelligent startup research and decision support.
